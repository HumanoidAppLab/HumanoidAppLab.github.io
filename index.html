REAL-WORLD VALUE

Making humanoids do real work.

We believe humanoid robots will soon be practical, affordable, and commonplace, and that this timeframe is only a few years away, not decades.

As production scales and improvements continue the humanoids themselves will become less expensive. So the real value will come from making them do real work, which means:

  • 03Interacting with your controls
  • 04Proving them out on real tasks
  • 05Training them to be repeatable and reliable
